# Journal selection

Recommended journal: The Computer Journal (Oxford University Press).

- Scope: computing, algorithms, combinatorial computation; matches a verified DP/exact-computation paper.
- Route: hybrid open access; standard (subscription) publication has no mandatory author publication fee.
- Optional OA APC exists (about USD 3,794 per OpenAlex record; verify current OUP price list).
- Preprint policy: author's original version may be posted publicly at any time.
- Author instructions: https://academic.oup.com/comjnl/pages/General_Instructions
- Publish with us: https://academic.oup.com/comjnl/pages/publish-with-us
- Self-archiving policy: https://academic.oup.com/pages/open-research/open-access/charges-licences-and-self-archiving/author-self-archiving-policy
