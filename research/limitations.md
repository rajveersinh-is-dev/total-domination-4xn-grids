# Limitations

- The exact formula is proved only for 4 x n grids, not for general m x n grids.
- The lower bound is verified computationally via the finite-state DP; a human-readable
  discharging or LP-dual proof is not included.
- The result is a modest contribution in combinatorial graph theory; it is not a
  breakthrough in the field.
- The manuscript has not been submitted, peer-reviewed, or accepted by any journal.
